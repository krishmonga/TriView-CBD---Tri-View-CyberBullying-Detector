Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Mitigating Cyberbullying with TriFuse: A Multi-View Model Fusing Lexical, Semantic, and Structural Text Features" for consideration in Social Network Analysis and Mining.

TriFuse is a multi-view deep learning model for detecting abusive text on social media. It fuses lexical (CNN), semantic (Transformer), and structural (BiLSTM) representations with attention-based fusion, evaluated on two public corpora (229,228 and 24,783 instances) with ablation studies and cross-validation.

The work fits SNAM's scope on social-media analytics, harmful-content detection, and network-scale text moderation. Source code and split configurations are available for reproducibility.

This manuscript is original and not under review elsewhere. All authors approve submission.
