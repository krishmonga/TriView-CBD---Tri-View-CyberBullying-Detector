Dear Editor,

We submit "Mitigating Cyberbullying with TriFuse: A Multi-View Model Fusing Lexical, Semantic, and Structural Text Features" for Online Social Networks and Media.

TriFuse detects abusive social-media text using parallel lexical, semantic, and structural encoders with attention fusion. We report dual-corpus experiments (Shahane 229,228 instances; Davidson 24,783 instances), baselines, and ablation studies.

This manuscript is original and approved by all authors.
