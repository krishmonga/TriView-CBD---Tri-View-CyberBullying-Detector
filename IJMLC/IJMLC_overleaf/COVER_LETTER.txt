Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled "Mitigating Cyberbullying with TriFuse: A Multi-View Model Fusing Lexical, Semantic, and Structural Text Features" for publication in the International Journal of Machine Learning and Cybernetics.

Cyberbullying detection is a text classification problem in which representation choice and fusion strategy strongly affect performance. Most existing systems rely on a single encoder (CNN, LSTM, or Transformer) or combine models through late ensemble voting. We propose TriFuse, which formulates the task as multi-view representation learning: lexical (CNN), semantic (Transformer), and structural (BiLSTM) branches train jointly on shared embeddings, and an attention-based fusion layer with learnable temperature assigns instance-dependent branch weights.

We evaluate TriFuse on two public corpora—the Shahane multi-platform dataset (229,228 posts) and the Davidson hate-speech corpus (24,783 tweets)—under matched preprocessing, splits, and training budgets. TriFuse ranks first among Random Forest, LightGBM, CNN, BiLSTM, and Tuned LSTM baselines under single-split and stratified 5-fold cross-validation. Ablation experiments and manual error analysis on misclassified posts are included.

We believe this work fits the scope of IJMLC because it addresses machine learning challenges in pattern recognition and multi-view fusion for text classification, with reproducible experiments across datasets and validation protocols.

This manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the submission.
